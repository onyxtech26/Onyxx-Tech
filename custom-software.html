Common questions

Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp.

Is custom software cheaper than a subscription?

Sometimes. Per-seat subscriptions look cheap at five users and painful at fifty, and they cost you every month forever. Custom is a larger one-off with a small hosting bill after. The answer depends on your headcount and how far your process sits from the off-the-shelf option. We will tell you if buying is the better call.

What if we already have a system we hate?

Very common. We can often build alongside it and move one process at a time, rather than a big-bang replacement that stops work for a week. Migrating your data across is part of the job, not an extra.

Who hosts it?

Usually your own cloud account so the bill and the access stay with you. We set it up and hand over the keys. Hosting for a typical internal tool is a modest monthly cost, not an enterprise licence.

What happens if we stop working with you?

You keep everything. Code in your repository, data in your database, accounts in your name, and documentation written for whoever comes next.
