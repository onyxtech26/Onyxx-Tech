What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers. An agent acts. A chatbot can tell a customer your opening hours; an agent can check your calendar, book the slot, write it to your system and send the confirmation. If the job ends with something changing in your business, you want an agent.

How long does it take to build an AI agent?

A first working agent on a narrow task is usually two to four weeks from the kickoff conversation. Broader multi-step agents that touch several systems take longer, mostly because of access and approvals on your side rather than the build itself.

Will it make things up?

Language models can. That is exactly why we scope agents to tasks with checkable outputs and put an approval step in front of anything that spends money, contacts a customer or edits a record. The agent proposes; a person confirms until it has proven itself.

Do we need our own OpenAI or Claude account?

Usually yes, and that is deliberate. The API account stays in your name, so the agent keeps running whoever maintains it later. We will set it up with you and keep the running cost visible rather than buried in a retainer.
