Privacy notice

Last updated 30 September 2026

Onyxx Tech is a software studio based in Malaysia. This notice explains what we collect through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What we collect

Only two things:

  • What you type into our contact form: your name, email, and optionally your phone number, company and a description of your project. We also record which page you were on when you sent it, so we know what you were reading.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: page views and referrers, collected by Vercel Web Analytics. This does not use cookies, does not follow you across other websites, and does not identify you personally.

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not run Facebook or Google advertising pixels, and we do not buy or sell contact data.

Why we collect it

The contact form exists so we can reply to you about your enquiry. That is the only thing we use it for. We will not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.

The usage statistics tell us which pages people read, so we can write better ones.

Where it is stored

Contact form submissions are stored in our Supabase database, hosted in the Singapore region, and are readable only by the two founders of Onyxx Tech. This website is hosted on Vercel.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as the conversation is live, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back to us. If you ask us to delete yours, we will, and we will confirm when it is done.

Who else sees it

Nobody outside Onyxx Tech, other than the service providers that host the infrastructure described above (Supabase and Vercel), who process it on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes.

Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 you may ask us to show you what personal data we hold about you, correct it if it is wrong, or delete it. Email onyxtech26@gmail.com and we will action it. There is no charge and you do not need to give a reason.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this notice changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be obvious rather than buried.

Contact

Questions about any of this go to onyxtech26@gmail.com.
