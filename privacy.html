Privacy notice

Last updated 30 October 2026 · Dikemas kini 30 Oktober 2026

This notice explains what personal data Onyxx Tech collects through this website, why, and the choices and rights you have under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). Baca notis ini dalam Bahasa Malaysia.

Who we are

Onyxx Tech (registration no. 202603038643 (003822277-T)) is a web design and software studio based in Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia. We are the data user responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: onyxtech26@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • What you enter in our contact form: your name, email address and message, and optionally your phone number, business name and the type of project. We also record which page you sent the form from.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: page views and referring websites, counted by Vercel Web Analytics. This does not use cookies and does not identify you.

If you contact us by WhatsApp or email instead, we receive the details you choose to send through that service.

Why we collect it

We use your contact details only to reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to discuss and deliver your project. We do not send marketing you did not ask for, and we do not sell or rent personal data. Usage statistics help us understand which pages are useful.

What is required and what is optional

Your name, email address and message are required so that we can reply. Your phone number and business name are optional. If you do not provide the required details, we will not be able to respond to your enquiry through the form.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Contact form submissions are stored in our Supabase database, hosted in Singapore, and can be read only by the Onyxx Tech team. The website is hosted by Vercel. These providers process data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes. We do not disclose your personal data to anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as the conversation is active and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you return to us. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your rights and choices

You may ask to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to limit how we use it, or to have it deleted. You may also withdraw your consent at any time. Email onyxtech26@gmail.com with your request or any complaint; there is no charge.

Security

Form data is sent over an encrypted connection and the database only allows our team to read submissions. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and will act promptly if something goes wrong.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update the date at the top of the page. If the English and Bahasa Malaysia versions differ, the English version applies.
