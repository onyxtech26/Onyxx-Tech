How we approach it

Good-looking is the minimum. It also has to work.

Plenty of websites in Malaysia look fine in a screenshot and fail where it counts: slow on mobile data, invisible on Google, and impossible to update.

Speed is a feature people feel.

Most sites in this market are heavy templates carrying plugins nobody uses. We build lean, so pages open quickly on a phone on mobile data, which is where your customers actually are.

Design that looks expensive, built to be cheap to run.

A distinctive site does not have to be a heavy one. We design from scratch for your business, then build it so there is very little to patch, break or pay to host.

Built so Google can read it.

Many modern sites render everything in JavaScript and are close to invisible in search. We put the content in the page, add structured data and treat being findable as part of the design.

Questions

Website design questions

Anything else, just ask. A conversation costs nothing.

How much does a website cost in Johor Bahru?

It depends on what the site has to do. The market runs from a few hundred ringgit for a filled-in template to five figures for a bespoke build. We sit in the middle and are upfront about it: you are paying for design and development time, not licences. Tell us the scope and we will give you a fixed price in writing before anything starts.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the number of pages and how quickly content and feedback come back. We agree a timeline with you in the quote, before any work starts, and show you progress along the way.

Do you build on WordPress?

Only if there is a reason to, for example if your team already knows it. Otherwise we prefer modern static or app-framework builds: faster, cheaper to host, and without the plugin-update treadmill that breaks sites nobody is watching.

Will I be able to update the content myself?

If you want to, yes. We put a simple admin panel behind the parts that change often, such as products, prices or news, and leave the rest as fast static pages. Not every site needs a CMS, and one you never log into is just another thing to keep patched.

Will my website show up on Google?

We build every site so search engines can read it: real text in the page, proper titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and fast load times, and we help you set up Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile. Nobody can honestly guarantee a ranking, but nothing on the technical side will be holding you back.

Can you also build a mobile app?

Yes. We build apps for iPhone and Android from one codebase, which keeps the cost far closer to one app than two. We have shipped cross-platform apps for retail, finance and education clients.

Do you work with businesses outside Johor Bahru?

Yes. We are based in Johor Bahru and happy to meet clients around Johor in person, and we work remotely with businesses elsewhere in Malaysia and overseas.
