Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost in Johor Bahru?

It depends on what the site has to do. The market runs from a few hundred ringgit for a filled-in template to five figures for a bespoke build. We sit in the middle and give you a fixed price in writing before any work starts, so the number never moves halfway through.

Do you only work with businesses in Johor Bahru?

No. We are based in JB and happy to meet clients around Johor in person, and we work remotely with businesses elsewhere in Malaysia and overseas. One of our website clients is a tour operator in Rome.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site we build is written so search engines can read it: real text in the page, proper titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and fast load times. We also help you set up Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile. No one can guarantee a ranking, but nothing on the technical side will be holding you back.

Can I update the website myself?

If you want to, yes. We add a simple admin panel to the parts that change often, like products, prices or news, and leave the rest as fast static pages. Or we can look after updates for you on a maintenance plan.

Do I own my website?

Yes. You own the code, the domain and the hosting accounts at the end of the project. If you ever want to move to another developer, you can take everything with you.

Do you build on WordPress?

Only when there is a good reason, for example if your team already uses it. Otherwise we build modern static or app-based sites: faster, cheaper to host, and without plugins that break when nobody is watching.
