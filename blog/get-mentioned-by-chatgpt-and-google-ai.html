Is your business in ChatGPT's answer? How to get mentioned by AI

More customers ask AI which shop to go to. Here is what those answers are built from, and the checklist we use to get our clients into them.

By Onyxx Tech26 November 20266 min read

Download as PDFFree. Save it, print it, share it with your team.

Ask ChatGPT for "the best bakery in Johor Bahru" and it names a few businesses. Google does something similar now: an AI answer sits above the normal results, and Google's AI Mode is available in Malaysia. If the AI does not mention you, that customer may never see your name. The good news is that AI answers are built from things you can control: your website, your Google Business Profile and your reviews.

Try it yourself first

Open ChatGPT, Gemini or Google and ask the questions your customers would ask:

  1. "Best [what you sell] in [your area]"
  2. "[What you sell] near [a landmark or taman close to you]"
  3. "Is [your business name] good?"

Write down who gets named. If your competitors appear and you do not, keep reading.

What AI answers are built from

AI tools do not have a secret list of businesses. They read what is published about you:

  • Your website: what you sell, where you are, who you serve, your prices and opening hours.
  • Your Google Business Profile: category, address, hours, photos, services.
  • Reviews and mentions: what customers write about you on Google and elsewhere, and whether other websites link to you.

If those sources are thin, vague or out of date, the AI has nothing to repeat.

The checklist we use for our clients

On your website

  • One clear sentence near the top: what you sell, for whom, and where. "Custom birthday cakes in Johor Bahru, with same-day pickup in Taman Molek" beats "Welcome to our website".
  • A page for each main product or service, written the way customers ask about it.
  • Your name, address and phone number exactly as on your Google profile, on every page.
  • Prices or price ranges where you can. AI tools like specifics.
  • A short FAQ that answers real questions: delivery areas, halal status, how to order, payment.
  • Fast pages that work on a phone.

On Google

  • A complete Google Business Profile. Google says customers are 70% more likely to visit a business with a complete profile.
  • The right primary category, real photos and up-to-date hours.

Reviews

  • Ask happy customers for reviews, and ask them to mention what they bought. "Their pandan cake for my son's birthday was perfect" tells the AI more than "Good service".
  • Reply to every review.

What not to do

  • Do not stuff keywords. Writing "best bakery JB" twenty times does not help, and reads badly to people.
  • Do not buy reviews. Google removes fake reviews, and AI tools learn from the same sources.
  • Do not expect overnight results. Changes are not instant: the AI has to find and read your updated pages first.

Where to start on Monday

  • Today: ask ChatGPT and Google the three questions above, and note who appears.
  • This week: rewrite the first sentence on your home page and check your Google profile is complete.
  • This month: ask ten happy customers for a review that mentions what they bought.

Questions people ask

Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT's answers?

Not in the normal answers. Google does show ads in AI Mode in Malaysia, but those are marked as sponsored and are separate from the AI's own recommendations.

Does a website still matter if AI answers everything?

More than before. The AI needs a source to quote, and your website is the one source you fully control.

How long does it take to show up?

There is no fixed timeline, and nobody can promise one. Each AI tool updates what it knows on its own schedule, so make the changes, then check again every few weeks.

Do social media posts count?

They can help people find you, but a website and a Google profile are what AI tools rely on most for business details like address, hours and services.
