This free tool does your boring admin: 5 automations to set up first

n8n connects the apps you already use, so orders, forms and reminders move by themselves. Here are the five automations we would set up first.

By Onyxx Tech26 November 20267 min read

Download as PDFFree. Save it, print it, share it with your team.

If someone in your business copies information from one app to another every day, that job can usually run by itself. n8n is a free workflow tool, with over 200,000 stars on GitHub, that connects the apps you already use: when something happens in one, it does something in another. Below is how it works, five automations worth setting up first, and the honest catch.

How it works

You build a workflow from blocks on a screen:

  1. A trigger: "a new email arrives", "someone fills in the website form", "every Monday at 9 am".
  2. Steps: "add a row to the Google Sheet", "send the team a message", "create a customer record".

n8n lists over 1,500 integrations, including Gmail, Google Sheets, Telegram and WhatsApp Business Cloud, so most small businesses can connect what they already use.

5 automations to set up first

1. New order email → spreadsheet → team alert Order confirmations from your website or marketplace get added to your orders sheet, and the team gets a message. Nobody retypes the order.

2. Website form → customer list Every enquiry from your contact form goes straight into your customer list or CRM, with the date and the page it came from.

3. Payment reminders Every morning, n8n checks for unpaid invoices that are due and sends a polite reminder.

4. Weekly report Every Monday, it collects last week's sales or orders into one summary and sends it to you.

5. Review request A day after an order is marked delivered, the customer gets a thank-you message with your Google review link.

The honest catch

n8n is free to run on your own server for your own business, under its Sustainable Use License. But "free" means someone has to:

  • set up the server and keep it updated and backed up,
  • connect each app (logins, permissions, API keys),
  • build and test the workflows, and fix them when an app changes.

If you have a technical person in the team, that is fine. If not, that is the part we set up for clients, so the automations keep running without anyone thinking about them.

What not to automate yet

  • Anything where the rule is still changing. Automate a process only when everyone does it the same way.
  • Anything that happens twice a year. The setup costs more than the time saved.

Where to start on Monday

  • List every task where someone copies information from one place to another.
  • Time it for a week.
  • Pick the one that takes the longest and happens most often. That is your first automation.

Questions people ask

Is n8n really free?

The self-hosted version is free for your own internal business use under its Sustainable Use License. n8n also sells a paid cloud version if you do not want to run a server.

Do I need to know how to code?

No for simple workflows: you connect blocks on a screen. Setting up the server and the app connections is easier with someone technical.

Can it send WhatsApp messages?

Yes, through WhatsApp's official Business Cloud API, which charges per message. It cannot use the normal WhatsApp Business app on your phone.

Is my data safe?

When you host it yourself, your data stays on your server. Keep the server updated, use strong passwords and back it up.
