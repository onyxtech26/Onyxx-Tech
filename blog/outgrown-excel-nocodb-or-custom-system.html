Three people, one Excel file, nobody knows which is right

When a spreadsheet is shared by the whole team, it starts to break. NocoDB is a free GitHub tool that turns it into a shared database. Here is when it is enough, and when you need a proper system.

By Onyxx Tech26 November 20267 min read

Download as PDFFree. Save it, print it, share it with your team.

orders_final.xlsx, orders_final_v2.xlsx, orders_final_REAL.xlsx. When three people work from three copies of the same file, nobody knows which one is right, and mistakes follow. You do not always need a big system to fix it. NocoDB, a free tool with over 65,000 stars on GitHub, turns a spreadsheet into a shared database that still looks like a spreadsheet. This post covers the signs you have outgrown Excel, what NocoDB does, and how to choose between Excel, a tool like NocoDB, or a custom system.

Signs you have outgrown Excel

  • More than one version of the same file exists.
  • Two people need to edit at the same time.
  • Someone has to ask "is this the latest?" every day.
  • Formulas break when someone sorts or adds a row.
  • Staff can see data they should not, like costs or salaries.
  • You cannot tell who changed what, or when.

Two or more of these, and it is time to look at something else.

What NocoDB does

According to its README, NocoDB gives you:

  • A spreadsheet view that your team already understands.
  • Other views of the same data: forms for adding records, a kanban board for status, a calendar, and a gallery.
  • Proper field types: links between tables, attachments, currency, formulas.
  • Logins: everyone works on the same data at the same time, with their own account.

It is free to run on your own server for your own business under its Sustainable Use License, and the company also offers a hosted version.

Excel, NocoDB or a custom system?

Excel / Google Sheets NocoDB (no-code database) Custom system
Best for One person, simple lists A team sharing records, forms and status Your exact process, rules and integrations
Cost Low Free software + a server or hosted plan A build, plus maintenance
Logins and permissions Basic Yes Exactly as you need
Rules ("can't confirm without stock") Formulas, easily broken Limited Built in
Connects to e-invoicing, payments, WhatsApp Manual Through automations Built in

A good path for many businesses: move from Excel to a tool like NocoDB, learn what you need, then build a custom system when the rules and integrations matter. Your data is already organised by then, which makes the move easier.

Where to start

  1. List the spreadsheets your team shares, and who edits each one.
  2. Pick the one that causes the most mistakes.
  3. Write down its columns and who needs to see what.
  4. Try it in NocoDB with sample data first, never with real customer details until logins and backups are set up.

Questions people ask

Is NocoDB really free?

The self-hosted version is free for your own internal business use under its Sustainable Use License. Hosted plans cost money.

Can I import my Excel file?

Yes, NocoDB can import spreadsheets. Clean up the columns first: one type of information per column.

Is it safe for customer data?

On your own server, with logins, permissions, updates and backups, yes. Treat customer data as personal data under the PDPA.

When should I go custom instead?

When you need your own rules (stock, approvals, pricing), connections to e-invoicing or payments, or a customer-facing part. That is where we usually come in.
