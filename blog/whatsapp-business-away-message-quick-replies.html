A customer messages at 10 pm. Nobody replies. Here is the free fix

Three free features in the WhatsApp Business app make sure no customer waits in silence. How to set them up in five minutes, with ready-made messages in English and Bahasa.

By Onyxx Tech26 November 20266 min read

Download as PDFFree. Save it, print it, share it with your team.

When a customer messages your business at night and hears nothing, many will not wait until morning. They message the next shop. The WhatsApp Business app already has three free features that fix this: away messages, greeting messages and quick replies. Here is what each one does, how to set it up, and messages you can copy.

1. Away message: replies for you when you are closed

An away message is sent automatically when a customer messages you outside the times you choose. According to WhatsApp's help page, you can send it always, on a custom schedule, or outside business hours (this uses the hours in your business profile), and choose who receives it: everyone, people not in your contacts, or everyone except chosen contacts. Your phone needs an internet connection for it to send.

Set it up: in the WhatsApp Business app, go to Business tools, then Away message, turn it on, choose the schedule and write your message.

English: Hi! Thanks for messaging [business]. We are closed now and will reply from 9 am tomorrow. To order, send us: item, quantity and pickup or delivery time. 🙏

BM: Hai! Terima kasih kerana menghubungi [business]. Kami sudah tutup dan akan membalas mulai 9 pagi esok. Untuk tempahan, hantar: item, kuantiti dan masa ambil atau penghantaran. 🙏

Asking for the order details in the away message means you wake up to complete orders, not "hi" messages.

2. Greeting message: the first thing new customers see

A greeting message is sent when a customer messages you for the first time, or after a long gap. It can include a link to your catalog, so people see your products straight away.

Set it up: Business tools, then Greeting message.

English: Hi, welcome to [business]! Here is our menu and prices: [catalog link]. What would you like to order today?

BM: Hai, selamat datang ke [business]! Ini menu dan harga kami: [catalog link]. Nak order apa hari ini?

3. Quick replies: answers in one second

Quick replies are saved messages for questions you answer every day. Type "/" in any chat and your list appears; the app also creates some from your business profile, such as your address and hours (WhatsApp help).

Set it up: Business tools, then Quick replies, then add one with a shortcut.

Good ones to save: your price list, address with a Google Maps link, bank details for payment, delivery areas and fees, and "thank you for your order".

One thing to watch

If you turn on Meta Business Agent (Meta's AI assistant for business chats), WhatsApp says it may stop some features working, including greeting and away messages. Check your settings if your away message stops sending.

Where to start today

  • Add your opening hours to your business profile.
  • Turn on the away message for outside business hours.
  • Save five quick replies for the questions you answer most.

Questions people ask

Are these features free?

Yes, they are part of the free WhatsApp Business app.

Do they work on the normal WhatsApp app?

No, only on the WhatsApp Business app.

Can I use these with an online shop or a system?

Yes. If your orders come in through WhatsApp's official Business API instead (for example from a website or a chatbot), the same ideas apply, set up in that system instead.

What if I get too many messages to answer by hand?

That is when an order form or a simple system helps: see how to stop retyping WhatsApp orders.
