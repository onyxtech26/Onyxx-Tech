One free inbox for every customer message: WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook

When messages are spread across five apps, some always slip through. Chatwoot puts them in one inbox your whole team can share. What it does, what it costs, and how to start.

By Onyxx Tech26 November 20266 min read

Download as PDFFree. Save it, print it, share it with your team.

Customers message you wherever is easiest for them: WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, the chat box on your website, email. When each channel lives in a different app, on different phones, some messages wait too long and some never get answered. Chatwoot is a free, open-source tool with over 40,000 stars on GitHub that puts all of them into one shared inbox.

What it does

  • One inbox for website live chat, email, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram and more (Chatwoot README).
  • A shared team view: everyone sees every conversation, so two people never answer the same customer.
  • Assignment: give each conversation to one person, so nothing is left for "someone else".
  • Saved replies for questions you answer every day.
  • Reports: how many conversations came in, and how long customers waited.

What it costs

  • Chatwoot itself: the core is free to run on your own server under the MIT licence (some enterprise features are licensed separately). Chatwoot also sells a hosted version if you do not want to run a server.
  • A server: a small cloud server, paid monthly.
  • WhatsApp: Chatwoot connects through WhatsApp's official Business API, not the WhatsApp Business app on your phone. Meta charges for some messages; check Meta's current pricing for Malaysia before you switch.
  • Setup time: connecting each channel, adding your team and saving replies.

Is it right for you?

It usually is when:

  • more than one person answers customers,
  • messages come in on three or more channels,
  • you have lost an order because a message was missed.

It probably is not when one person answers everything on one phone. The free features of the WhatsApp Business app may be enough: see away messages and quick replies.

How to set it up

  1. Decide where it runs: Chatwoot's hosted version, or your own server.
  2. Connect your channels: website chat (a small code snippet), email, Facebook and Instagram pages, then WhatsApp through the Business API.
  3. Add your team and decide who handles what.
  4. Save your top ten replies.
  5. Agree one rule: every conversation gets an owner within 10 minutes during opening hours.

Questions people ask

Can I keep using WhatsApp on my phone?

Not with the same number once it is connected to the Business API. Plan the switch, and tell your team.

Is my customer data safe?

On your own server, the data stays with you. Keep it updated and backed up, and treat customer messages as personal data under the PDPA.

Does it have AI replies?

Chatwoot has an AI agent feature (Captain) for common questions. Start with saved replies first; add AI once you know your common questions.
