Your WhatsApp button is losing you customers. Here is the fix

A button hidden in the footer costs you enquiries. Make it stay on screen and open WhatsApp with a ready-made message, using one link.

By Onyxx Tech26 November 20265 min read

Download as PDFFree. Save it, print it, share it with your team.

Most small business websites in Malaysia have a WhatsApp link somewhere. The problem is where. When it is hidden at the bottom of the page, people who want to ask a question have to hunt for it, and many give up. Two changes fix most of this: a button that stays on screen, and a link that opens WhatsApp with a message already written.

Fix 1: a button that stays on screen

  • Fixed in the bottom corner, visible while the visitor scrolls, on every page.
  • Big enough to tap with a thumb on a phone.
  • Not covering anything important, like the "add to cart" button or the cookie notice.
  • Labelled if you can: "WhatsApp us" or "Ask us on WhatsApp" works better than an icon alone.

Fix 2: a link with a ready-made message

WhatsApp's click-to-chat link can open a chat with a message already typed. The customer only presses send.

The format is:

https://wa.me/<your number>?text=<your message>
  • Your number in full international format, without "+", spaces, dashes or the first 0. For 012-345 6789 in Malaysia, that is 60123456789.
  • Your message written with %20 for each space (or use a link builder that does this for you).

Example:

https://wa.me/60123456789?text=Hi,%20I'd%20like%20a%20quote%20for%20a%20birthday%20cake

Use a different message on each page

A different message per page tells you exactly what the customer is asking about:

Page Prefilled message
Home Hi, I'd like to ask about your services
Birthday cakes Hi, I'd like a quote for a birthday cake
Delivery info Hi, do you deliver to my area?
Contact Hi, I'd like to visit your shop

You save a question every time, and you can see which pages bring enquiries.

A link builder you can copy

Ask Claude (or any AI assistant) to make your links:

Make WhatsApp click-to-chat links for my business. My number is [012-345 6789, Malaysia]. Create one wa.me link with a prefilled message for each of these pages: [list your pages and what customers usually ask there]. URL-encode the messages and give me a table.

Then test every link on your own phone before you publish it.

Where to start today

  • Open your website on your phone. Can you find the WhatsApp button in two seconds?
  • Make it stay on screen.
  • Replace the plain link with a prefilled one.

Questions people ask

Does this work with the WhatsApp Business app?

Yes. The link opens a chat with your number in whichever WhatsApp the customer uses.

Can I track which page a message came from?

Yes, by using a different prefilled message on each page, as above.

Will a floating button slow my website down?

A simple button and link add almost nothing. Heavy chat widgets that load extra scripts can, so test your speed after adding one.

Do I need a developer?

On most website builders you can add a floating button yourself. On a custom site, it is a small change for whoever maintains it.
