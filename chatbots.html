Chatbots

Answer every customer, at 2am, in one second.

In Malaysia the first message a customer sends you is on WhatsApp. A chatbot decides whether that message gets answered in one second or the next morning — and by then they have asked someone else.

#01

Fast beats clever.

Customers forgive a bot that says “let me get a human”. They do not forgive silence for eleven hours. The single biggest win is usually replying instantly to the eight questions you already answer forty times a week.

#02

A bot that cannot hand over is a wall.

Every bot we build has a clean escape hatch to a real person, with the conversation history attached so the customer never repeats themselves. The bot handles volume; your team handles the ones that are worth their time.

#03

Build it on your own data, not generic answers.

Prices, stock, opening hours, delivery areas — a bot is only useful if it answers from what is actually true today. We wire it to your real source of truth rather than a script that goes stale in a month.

What you get
  • WhatsApp, Telegram or on-site web chat — or all three at once
  • Answers pulled from your live data, not a hardcoded script
  • Lead capture that lands in your inbox or CRM
  • Clean handover to a human with full conversation history
  • Malay and English handling in the same conversation
  • A dashboard of what people actually asked, so you learn from it
