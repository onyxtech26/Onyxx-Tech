How much does a WhatsApp chatbot cost in Malaysia?

There are two costs: the build, and the running cost. The running cost is the WhatsApp Business API itself, billed per conversation through a provider, plus model usage. For a typical small business that comes to roughly RM150–450 a month. The build is a one-off and depends entirely on how much your bot needs to know. We quote both separately so you can see which is which.

Do I need the official WhatsApp Business API?

For anything automated at scale, yes. The free WhatsApp Business app is fine for manual replies but is not built for bots, and unofficial workarounds risk your number being banned. We set you up properly through an approved provider under your own business account.

Can it talk in Malay?

Yes, including switching mid-conversation, which is how people type here. Modern models handle Manglish and code-switching far better than the rule-based bots most businesses tried a few years ago.

What happens when it does not know the answer?

It says so and passes the conversation to a human rather than guessing. We would much rather a bot admits a gap than invents a delivery date.
