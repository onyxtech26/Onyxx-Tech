Cookie notice

Last updated 30 September 2026

Short version: this site sets no tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner to dismiss.

What we do not use

No advertising cookies, no third-party tracking cookies, no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no cross-site profiling of any kind. Nothing on this site follows you to another website.

What we do store on your device

Two small entries in your browser's local storage, which is not a cookie and is never sent to our server:

  • Your theme preference (dark or light), so the site does not flash the wrong one at you on your next visit.
  • Whether you have seen the intro animation this session.

Both stay on your device. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings, and the site will work exactly as before.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It is cookie-less and aggregates data without identifying individual visitors. See our privacy notice for detail.

Embedded content

We load fonts from Google Fonts and a small number of scripts from public CDNs (jsDelivr, unpkg) to render the site. These providers may log the request as part of serving the file, in the ordinary way any web request is logged. We do not pass them any information about you.
