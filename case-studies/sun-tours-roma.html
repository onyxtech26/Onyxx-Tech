The brief

Sun Tours, a tour operator in Rome, sells self-guided audio tours of the city. Visitors get each tour as a simple link they open on their phone, so the website has to explain the experience clearly and make it easy to get in touch.

What we built

A website that presents the tours and the experience, with an admin login so the Sun Tours team can manage things themselves. Supabase runs behind the scenes and the domain is on GoDaddy, both in the client’s name.

Ongoing care

We continue to look after it on a maintenance plan: updates, fixes and small changes, handled by the people who built it.

Ownership

As with every project we take on, the code and the accounts belong to the client at the end of it, so they are never locked in to us.
