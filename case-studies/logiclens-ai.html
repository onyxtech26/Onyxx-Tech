The brief

LogicLens.AI is an immersive mathematical learning application designed for schools. It uses an AI-powered 'virtual guide' and interactive 360° tours to help students explore and visualize mathematical concepts like algebra and geometry in a fun and engaging way, anywhere they are.

What we built

A Flutter learning app for schools combining an AI guide with interactive 360° tours, using Supabase for content and both OpenAI and Gemini models depending on the task.

The stack

Flutter, Dart, Supabase, OpenAI, Gemini — chosen to fit the job rather than to pad a list. Every project we take on is scoped so the client owns the code and the accounts at the end of it.
