The brief

Our own product: a trading journal for forex traders to record trades, review their history and spot patterns in their performance.

What we built

A Vue and Supabase app that runs on the web and on mobile from one codebase, syncing live, with performance metrics and charts calculated from the trade history rather than typed in by hand.

Ownership

As with every project we take on, the code and the accounts belong to the client at the end of it, so they are never locked in to us.
