The brief

A professional website for a multinational group, with a clean corporate layout that presents its operating areas (energy, technology, manufacturing and finance) and its featured projects.

What we built

A corporate site structured around the group’s divisions, written as hand-built markup rather than a page-builder theme, so it stays fast and has no plugins to keep patching.

Ownership

As with every project we take on, the code and the accounts belong to the client at the end of it, so they are never locked in to us.
