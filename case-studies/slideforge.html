SlideForge

An offline Windows app that builds branded social media carousels from ready-made templates.

Client
Onyxx Tech product
Type
Offline Windows app
Year
2026

The brief

Making a polished carousel post usually means opening design software and rebuilding the same layout every time. SlideForge turns that into a few clicks, and works without an internet connection.

What we built

A Windows desktop app with one-click templates (problem and solution, listicle, step by step, myth-busting, before and after, FAQ and more), a slide editor with brand settings, import, undo, preview and auto-save, and export of a single slide or the whole carousel.

Why we built it

We build our own products as well as client work. It keeps our skills sharp and lets us test ideas properly before we recommend them to anyone else.
