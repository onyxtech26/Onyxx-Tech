MPT Watch Sales Bot

A chat assistant that does commission and discount maths for retail staff on the shop floor.

Client
Million Precision Time
Type
Chatbot · Retail
Year
2026
Built with
Node.jsBot automation

The brief

Retail staff at Million Precision Time were working out watch prices, commissions and discounts by hand at the counter, which was slow and caused mistakes at the point of sale.

What we built

A conversational bot for floor staff that does the commission and discount arithmetic in the chat window, so nobody is doing percentage maths in front of a customer.

Ownership

As with every project we take on, the code and the accounts belong to the client at the end of it, so they are never locked in to us.
