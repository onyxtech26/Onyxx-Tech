Automations

Stop paying people to copy and paste.

Every business has a person who spends an hour a day moving the same information from one place to another. That hour is the cheapest thing you will ever buy back.

#01

Automate the boring middle, not the judgement.

The best candidates are the steps where nobody makes a decision — moving an order into the system, chasing an unpaid invoice, filing a receipt. Leave the judgement calls with your team and take the typing off them.

#02

If it breaks silently, it is worse than manual.

An automation that quietly stops is a disaster you find out about weeks later. Everything we build reports failures loudly — to a WhatsApp group, an email, wherever you will actually see it — and retries safely rather than half-finishing.

#03

Measure the hours before and after.

We ask you to time the process before we touch it. Without that number nobody can say whether the automation paid for itself, and the whole argument turns into a feeling.

What you get
  • Connections between the tools you already use — no migration required
  • Scheduled jobs: reports, reminders, reconciliations, backups
  • Document and receipt handling with data pulled out automatically
  • Failure alerts that reach a human, with safe retries
  • Self-hosted n8n on your own server, or a custom script where that is simpler
  • A before-and-after estimate of hours saved
